Units

Counting hashes in base 2

Hashu vouchers count hashes in TiH (tebihash, 240 hashes). The mining industry quotes hashrate in base 10, as TH/s, PH/s and EH/s. Pool share accounting and Cashu tokens both work in powers of two. TiH is the unit where those two meet without rounding, and this page shows how it converts to everything else.

The short version

A hash

One attempt at a block: a double SHA-256 of an 80-byte block header with one nonce. Hashrate is hashes per second. A Hashu voucher is a quantity of hashes, so it has no time in it.

1 TiH = 240 hashes

1,099,511,627,776 hashes

About 10% more than a trillion (1012). Exactly 256 difficulty-1 shares.

1 THh ≈ 3,274 TiH

3,274.18 TiH

One TH/s for one hour is 3.6 × 1015 hashes. It is not a whole number of TiH, and that is the problem TiH solves.

Two number systems

Base 10 · the industry

Hashrate is sold in SI prefixes

Miners, pools, rental markets and hashprice indexes use the metric prefixes: tera (1012), peta (1015) and exa (1018).

  • A Bitaxe does about 1.2 TH/s, a modern ASIC around 200 TH/s.
  • The network runs at hundreds of EH/s.
  • Hashprice is quoted per TH/s per day or per PH/s per day, as in $/PH/s/Day.
Base 2 · the plumbing

Shares and Cashu are powers of two

Shares. A pool counts work in shares. A difficulty-1 share takes 232 hashes on average, and a share at difficulty D takes D × 232. Pools usually set D to a power of two, like 512, 65,536 or 220.

Cashu. A Cashu mint signs one key per power of two. Every token amount is a sum of 1, 2, 4, 8 and so on, so any amount splits into at most one note of each size.

Why not count in THh?

Hashu's first mint did count vouchers in THh (terahash-hours). It worked for selling, but every share the proxy delivered became an awkward decimal fraction of a THh, and the binary pieces never added back up to round numbers.

The THh column never ends, because 3.6 × 1015 = 216 × 9 × 514: the factors of 3 and 5 in a THh can't be built from powers of two. In TiH, every share of difficulty 256 or more is a whole number, and smaller shares are exact binary fractions. So:

Exact metering

The proxy adds up share difficulty and divides by 256. The running total of delivered work is exact, with nothing lost to rounding as millions of shares arrive.

Exact change

When a redemption stops early, the change is the undelivered TiH, signed as fresh vouchers. It breaks cleanly into Cashu's power-of-two notes.

Same ladder

Hashu's keyset has one key for each of 20 through 240 TiH. A note of 2n TiH is exactly 2n+8 difficulty-1 shares, so token sizes and share sizes sit on the same ladder.

The "i" comes from the binary prefixes standard (IEC 80000-13), the same one behind GiB versus GB on a disk. Tebi is 240, pebi 250, exbi 260. Each step is 1,024 times the last, a little more than the 1,000 of tera, peta and exa.

Worked conversions

A Bitaxe for a day

A 1.2 TH/s Bitaxe running for 24 hours:

hashes1.2 × 1012 × 86,400 = 1.0368 × 1017 in TiH1.0368 × 1017 ÷ 240 ≈ 94,296 TiH in PiH94,296 ÷ 1,024 ≈ 92.1 PiH check1.2 THd × 78,580.3 = 94,296 TiH

How an amount splits into notes

A Cashu wallet holds one proof per power of two in an amount's binary form. 94,296 TiH (the Bitaxe day) is 10111000001011000 in binary, so it is seven notes, one for each 1:

216 = 65,536214 = 16,384213 = 8,192212 = 4,09626 = 6424 = 1623 = 8

65,536 + 16,384 + 8,192 + 4,096 + 64 + 16 + 8 = 94,296. Each note is a whole number of difficulty-256 shares, which no note in a THh-based keyset could be.

In Cashu wallets

Any Cashu wallet

Hashu vouchers are ordinary Cashu tokens in a custom unit, tih. A stock wallet such as Cashu.me shows the plain integer, so a 32 PiH voucher reads as 32,768 tih. It can hold and send them, but it can't redeem them as hashrate.

The Hashu wallet

app.hashu.io lets you pick a display unit (TiH, PiH, EiH, THh, PHh, THd, PHd, TH/s/Day or PH/s/Day) and shows prices in the same unit. It redeems straight to your pool.

A precise footnote: the difficulty-1 target is 0xFFFF × 2208, so a difficulty-1 share really takes 2256 ÷ (0xFFFF × 2208 + 1) ≈ 4,295,032,833 hashes on average, 0.0015% more than 232. Pools and miners all count a share as D × 232 hashes, and so does Hashu.
