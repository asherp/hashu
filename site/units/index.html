Counting hashes in base 2

Hashu vouchers count hashes in TiH (240 hashes). Shares and Cashu tokens are powers of two; the mining industry quotes base-10 TH/s. TiH is where the two meet without rounding.

Why a binary unit

Base 10 · the industry

Miners, pools and rental markets quote TH/s, PH/s and EH/s (1012, 1015, 1018). Hashprice is per TH/s/Day or PH/s/Day.

Base 2 · the plumbing

A difficulty-D share is D × 232 hashes, and pools pick D as a power of two. A Cashu mint signs one key per power of two, so every amount is a sum of 1, 2, 4, 8…

A THh (3.6 × 1015 = 216 × 9 × 514 hashes) can't be built from powers of two, so shares never come out even in THh. In TiH they do: delivered work is exactly Σ difficulty ÷ 256, change after an early stop is exact, and a 2n TiH note is exactly 2n+8 difficulty-1 shares. The "i" is the IEC binary prefix, as in GiB: each step is 1,024×.
