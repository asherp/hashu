Open source · Cashu · Stratum

Sell hashes, earn sats.

Hashu turns hashrate into Chaumian hashrate vouchers. A Hashu mint issues terahash-hours (THH) as Cashu tokens backed only by the hashrate of its miners, and the only way to redeem them is as real work delivered to the holder's pool. No bitcoin is held for users and none is paid out.

Demo mint on mainnet at mint.hashu.io. Run your own to sell your hashrate.

The problem

A typical ecash mint

  • Holds users' bitcoin and pays it back out on demand.
  • That is custody and transmission of money, with the regulatory weight that comes with it.
  • Tokens are claims on the operator's BTC reserves.

A Hashu mint

  • Sells a service: terahash-hours of mining computation.
  • Never pays out bitcoin. Tokens are redeemed only as hashrate.
  • Tokens are backed only by the miners behind the mint.

How it works

1

Buy

Pay a mainnet Lightning invoice, or pay in USDT. Dollars go to hashrate and on to bitcoin without an exchange.

2

Hold

Receive THH as Chaumian vouchers (Cashu tokens). Blind signatures mean the mint can't link who bought to who redeems.

3

Redeem

Melt the tokens with a pool URL and worker name. The mint's Stratum proxy points its miners at your pool until the work is delivered.

4

Audit

Every share credited to a redemption goes into its own Merkle tree. Holders can fetch the root and inclusion proofs to check the work that reached their pool.

1 THH = 1 TH/s for one hour = 3.6 × 1015 hashes.

Run your own mint

Self-hosted software, not a service

Anyone with hashrate can run Hashu and sell it as vouchers: one binary runs the Cashu mint, the Lightning and USDT payment rails, the Stratum proxy and an operator dashboard. Pricing follows the live hashrate rental market.

Built on open pieces

Rust on the Cashu Development Kit (cdk), mainnet Lightning through phoenixd, and Tether's Mining Development Kit for the hardware side. Dual-licensed MIT / Apache-2.0.
