Enter the hashrate you plan to run in each coming difficulty epoch (2,016 blocks, about two weeks): rigs coming online or going away. Each epoch starts out filled with your live average; only the epochs you change are saved, with your dashboard key. Future epochs your horizon reaches sell against the plan, less what is already sold into them; epochs without a plan sell on the live average. The current epoch always sells on what your rigs measure, and a plan freezes when its epoch starts. The plan is public at /v1/hashu/plan, next to what each epoch delivered according to your hourly reliability record.
