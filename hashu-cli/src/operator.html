    From accepted shares, in 30 s slots. Grey = offline.

    Estimated from accepted shares: each share of difficulty D is D × 232 hashes. A rig's total work is exact at each share and grows steadily between shares, so it is read off every 30 s and each slot's rate is the work in it ÷ 30 s; a slot sharing a gap between two shares gets its part of it. A rig does no work while it is disconnected (grey, Offline when no rig is connected) or while the daemon is down (light grey): the first share after a reconnect counts from the reconnect, not across the outage. The 1 and 5 min averages spread each slot's work over a centred triangular window that wide at half height, only onto time the rig was connected, so the area under the line (rate × time) is exactly the work of the shares. The newest slots wait for each connected rig's next share. Each rig name (the Stratum user after its first dot; rigs sharing a name add up) is a band; the bands stack to the total line. Includes hashrate sent to the default pool and to redeemers.

    Planned vs delivered

    EpochRanPlannedDeliveredOf planHours in record

    Enter the hashrate you plan to run in each coming difficulty epoch (2,016 blocks, about two weeks): a build-out, rigs coming online or going away. Pick the unit above: a rate (TH/s, PH/s) or the work of a whole epoch (THh, PHh, THd, PHd, TiH; 1 THd is 1 TH/s for a day). Each epoch starts out filled with your live average × two weeks; only the epochs you change are saved. Future epochs then sell against the plan × the part of the epoch your horizon covers, less what is already sold into them. Epochs without a plan sell on the live average. The current epoch always sells on what your rigs measure, and a plan freezes when its epoch starts. A cut is refused if the epoch would owe more than it can deliver. The plan is public at /v1/hashu/plan, next to what each epoch delivered according to your hourly reliability record: buyers price the promise by how well you keep it.

    Supply: an hourly snapshot taken right after each stock top-up, then live now. Each band is its TiH over the hours it covers, shown as TH/s, so a day's stock and a two-week epoch share one scale. At a top-up the bands add up to the supply line; above it means oversold (a top-up never takes back stock already listed). Right of now: the current stock (faded), then future epochs on sale. Hashes over time: running totals since the chart's first hour. Mined is all work your rigs did, from the hourly reliability record. Operator only, unfiltered: nothing here is public yet.

    Hashrate capacity

    Time-limited hashrate

    HashrateTH/sLeftTiH

    Hashrate on this proxy that stops at a known time (a rental from anywhere, a borrowed machine). It comes out of the live average now, and sells only until it ends: TH/s × hours left, within this epoch's part of the horizon. Add it while it is hashing; the end time is in your browser's time zone.

    Capacity = MRR rentals and time-limited hashrate (TH/s × hours left) + what is left of the live stock. Allocated + available = total. Mint quotes beyond what is available are refused, so with neither nothing is sold (checkout shows Sold out). Off sells uncapped.

    Live hashrate

    off
    hours

    Rigs mining on this proxy for this mint (your own rigs, e.g. a Bitaxe) back a stock of TiH, the same rule supplier mints sell by. Every hour it is topped up to what the rigs produce within the horizon: their average hashrate (EWMA, half-life = horizon ÷ 2) × the horizon, less what the mint owes beyond its rentals. In between, only sales take it down, so the quantity on sale holds still while the hashrate moves; the checkout's delivery ETA moves instead. A top-up never takes away stock already listed; changing the horizon starts a new batch. Hashrate rented rigs already declare is not counted twice. Rigs that only mine to their home pool, or serve a supplier's mint, are not counted.

    Heat produced

    estimate
    J/TH

    Miners turn almost all their power into heat, so heat ≈ work × J/TH; 1 TiH is 240 hashes ≈ 1.1 TH, so 1 TiH at E J/TH is about E × 1.1 J, and an hour of 1 TH/s (≈ 3,274 TiH) is E ÷ 1000 kWh. J/TH comes from each rental's rig name: a model (S21+, S21XP Hydro, S19j Pro…), else a make (Whatsminer, Antminer/ANT, Avalon) at a typical current machine, else the default above; weighted by TH/s.
