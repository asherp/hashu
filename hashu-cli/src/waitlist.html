Want a hosted mint?

Sell your hashrate as Chaumian hashrate vouchers from a mint of your own, without running a server. Point your rigs at one Stratum endpoint and get a mint at yourname.this-mint. Hosted mints aren't open yet: leave one way to reach you and you'll hear when they are.

  • Your own mint, your own priceEach miner gets a separate mint and sets their own ask. Vouchers from your mint are backed by your rigs only.
  • Buyers pay you directlySales settle straight to your own wallet. The hosting operator never holds your earnings.
  • Hashrate, not moneyVouchers are redeemed only as work delivered to the holder's pool. Between orders your rigs keep mining to your home pool.
  • Or run it yourselfHashu is open source. Self-host a mint today if you'd rather not wait.
