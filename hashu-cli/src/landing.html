How it works

1

Buy

Choose how many terahash-hours you want and pay a mainnet Lightning invoice, or send USDT. The mint is priced live from the hashrate rental market.

2

Hold

You receive THH as Cashu ecash: blind-signed bearer tokens in the thh unit. Keep them, send them to a friend, or redeem them right away.

3

Redeem

Melt the tokens with your pool URL and worker name. The mint's proxy redirects its miners to your pool until the work you paid for has been delivered, share by share.

1 THH = 1 TH/s for one hour = 3.6 × 1015 hashes. Redeem to any pool, including Testnet4.

Why Hashu

It sells computation, not money

A Hashu mint never pays out bitcoin. Its tokens are backed only by the hashrate of the miners behind it, and the only way out is as work delivered to your pool. That keeps the operator selling a service rather than holding and transmitting other people's money.

Private by construction

Cashu blind signatures mean the mint cannot link the tokens it issued to the tokens it later redeems. Who paid, who holds, and whose pool the hashrate lands on stay separate.

Dollars to bitcoin, no exchange

Pay in USDT, receive hashrate, mine bitcoin into your own pool account.

USD₮→THH→₿

Verifiable delivery

Each redemption gets its own Merkle tree of the shares it was credited, with audit endpoints so a holder can check the work that reached their pool.
