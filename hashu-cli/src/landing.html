Hashu is a Cashu mint fused with a mining proxy. It sells real hashes, counted in TiH (tebihash, 240 hashes), as Chaumian hashrate vouchers. Pay with Lightning, USDT or USDC, hold the tokens in any Cashu wallet, and redeem them whenever you like by pointing hashrate at your own pool.

How it works

1

Buy

Choose how many hashes you want and pay a mainnet Lightning invoice, or send USDT or USDC. The mint is priced live from the hashrate rental market.

2

Hold

You receive TiH as Chaumian vouchers: blind-signed Cashu tokens in the tih unit. Keep them, send them to a friend, or redeem them right away.

3

Redeem

Redeem the tokens with your pool URL and worker name. The mint's proxy redirects its miners to your pool until the work you paid for has been delivered, share by share.

1 TiH (tebihash) = 240 hashes = 256 difficulty-1 shares. 1 TH/s for one hour ≈ 3,274 TiH. Prices are per PiH (1,024 TiH), and purchases and redemptions start at 1 PiH. Redeem to any Stratum V1 or V2 pool that gives you a worker name. Why TiH, and how it converts.

Why Hashu

It sells computation, not money

A Hashu mint never pays out bitcoin. Its tokens are backed only by the hashrate of the miners behind it, and the only way out is as work delivered to your pool. That keeps the operator selling a service rather than holding and transmitting other people's money.

Private by construction

Cashu blind signatures mean the mint cannot link the tokens it issued to the tokens it later redeems. Who paid, who holds, and whose pool the hashrate lands on stay separate.

Dollars to bitcoin, no exchange

Pay in USDT or USDC, receive hashrate, mine bitcoin into your own pool account.

A public reliability record

Every hour the mint signs its hardest shares sent to redeemers' pools, posts them on Nostr and timestamps them on Bitcoin, so anyone can check its reliability record. Share proofs for your own redemption are on the roadmap.

Who it's for

Lightning node runners: free up inbound, get private coins

Paying a Hashu mint's Lightning invoice spends outbound liquidity from your channels and frees up inbound capacity, much like a Loop Out. Point the hashrate you bought at your own pool account and the pool pays you on-chain in freshly mined coins with no transaction history. Blind signatures mean the mint can't link the invoice you paid to the pool the work went to, so those coins aren't tied to your public channels.

Anyone who wants bitcoin on-chain

Cashu mints almost always pay out over Lightning only, and very few support on-chain. A Hashu mint never pays out bitcoin at all. Instead, the hashrate you redeem mines at the pool you choose, and the pool pays your bitcoin address directly. Buy with Lightning, USDT or USDC and end up with self-custodied bitcoin on-chain.

Good to know: what your pool pays depends on network difficulty, pool luck and pool fees, so it isn't a fixed rate. How fast the work is delivered depends on the mint, so check the mint's estimated delivery time. Pools pay out once your balance passes their payout threshold. At low volume, a purchase and a redemption close together in time can still hint that they're linked.
