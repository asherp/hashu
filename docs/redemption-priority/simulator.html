Hash rate is in TH/s. Orders, deliveries and the water level are in TH/s·h, one terahash per second sustained for an hour (3.6 million TH). The split is recomputed once a minute, so each minute delivers hash rate ÷ 60 TH/s·h. The mint is modelled as one pooled hash rate that switches between orders instantly; rented rigs and switching delays come later.

New orders keep arriving at random, sized 5 to 100 TH/s·h. Load is average demand as a share of hash rate; above 100% the backlog grows without limit.

Rule: a lone order fills straight up. Orders arriving in the same tick share one top, set at the water level plus the largest of their sizes and every open order's remaining amount. Existing tops never move.
