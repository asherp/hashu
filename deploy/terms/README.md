# Terms of sale

Markdown terms the mint serves at `/terms/` when `HASHU_TERMS_FILE` points
at one (compose mounts this folder at `/etc/hashu/terms`), and links from
the checkout's "I agree to the seller's terms of sale" box.

`seller-terms.md` is Hashu's own: the terms Pembroke Creative, LLC sells
vouchers under at mint.hashu.io. If you run your own mint, you are its
seller: write your own terms here and point `HASHU_TERMS_FILE` at them.
The git history of this folder is the record of every version.
